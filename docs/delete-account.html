Delete your MatchVictorious account

In the app: More → Settings → Delete account → confirm.

On the web: sign in below with the email and password you use for MatchVictorious, and the account is deleted immediately.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email and password), profile, approximate location, preferences
  • Your photos (database records and stored files)
  • Your likes and passes, matches, chat messages, blocks and call records

What is kept

  • Safety reports filed by or about you are kept for trust & safety and legal purposes. Your account link is removed; a snapshot of the reported name and bio is kept with reports about you.
  • Data imported from other dating apps was never uploaded. Remove it on your phone with Settings → Delete All Data, or by uninstalling.
  • Backups expire on our provider's backup schedule (up to 7 days on paid plans).
