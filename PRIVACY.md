# MatchVictorious Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-20_

- **What is collected:** nothing by us. The app processes only the export files you choose to import.
- **Where data goes:** nowhere. The app declares no INTERNET permission and cannot transmit data. Parsing happens on your device.
- **Storage:** imports are encrypted with AES-256-GCM (Jetpack Security EncryptedFile) using a key held in the Android Keystore. Android backup and device-transfer are disabled.
- **Credentials:** the app never asks for or stores your dating-platform passwords.
- **Other apps:** no automation, accessibility services, notification access or background sync.
- **Third-party data:** exports may contain other members' personal data. It stays on your device; please keep it private.
- **Fleet API:** an app holding the signature permission can request aggregate counts (number of imports, matches, messages), never content.
- **Deletion:** Settings → Delete All Data permanently erases every import, preference and cache. Uninstalling also erases everything.
- **Affiliation:** not affiliated with or endorsed by Tinder, Hinge, Bumble, OkCupid, Match, Plenty of Fish, Grindr, Feeld, Coffee Meets Bagel or Badoo.
